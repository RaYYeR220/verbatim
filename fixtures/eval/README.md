# Ledgerly interview benchmark (SYNTHETIC, labelled)
**What:** 8 WebVTT interviews (`interviews/p1–p8.vtt`, ~1.3k words / ~10 min each) on *"Why do new teams stall during their first month?"* for **Ledgerly**, a fictional team-expense & receipts SaaS. People are listed in `participants.json`.
**Synthetic:** every person, company and line is invented for evaluation. Never present these quotes as real customer research.
**Ground truth:** `answer-key.json` has 7 planted themes: T1–T5 shared (5/4/4/3/2 participants), **T6 minority** (exactly 1: p7, screen reader) and **T7 contradiction** (2 love the mobile app, 2 find it unusable). Each theme has verbatim `evidence` (3–6, ≤2 per participant) plus `extraEvidence`. There are also 2 **decoys** (mileage/GPS, foreign currency) that nobody raises.
**Text model:** strip `<v …>` tags and join cue bodies with `\n` per transcript. Every key quote is an exact substring of one cue spoken by that participant, and appears in no other transcript.
**Scoring:** (1) *theme recall*: a predicted theme matches a key theme if one of its cited spans overlaps an evidence/extraEvidence span by ≥50% of characters; (2) *quote fidelity*: share of cited quotes that are exact substrings of the cited transcript; (3) *speaker attribution*: share of quotes credited to the participant who actually said them (not the interviewer); (4) *minority-theme recall*: T6 is found; (5) *decoy false-positive rate*: predicted themes that match D1/D2. For T7, also check that both stances are reported.
**Verify:** `python -I scripts/data/verify_key.py` checks every evidence quote against the transcripts.
